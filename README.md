## The entry

> The lawmaker who spent the morning grilling AI executives about alignment spent the afternoon asking IT why renaming his bill still hasn't turned it into a PDF.

160 characters, one sentence. The setup is the lawmaker's authority — a morning grilling AI executives about alignment — and the turn undercuts it with the file he cannot convert: he believes renaming his bill turns it into a PDF. The punch lands on the last words, and the detail (renaming a file to convert it) is a specific, unexpected beat rather than a worn tech-illiteracy cliche.
